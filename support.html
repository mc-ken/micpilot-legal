Support

Email szmcken168@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the developer — there is no support team, just one person who wrote the thing. Please include what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

The phone page will not open

  • Check the link carefully: it should end with # followed by your six-character pairing code (for example https://relay.micpilot.tech/#QPBM9J). Copy it fresh from the extension popup rather than retyping it.
  • Try a different network once — some mobile networks block things their office Wi-Fi does not.
  • If the page opens but says "Relay not connected", pull the page down to refresh it; the connection is restored automatically a moment later.

The microphone button does nothing

  • Open the page in Safari (or your normal browser), not inside WeChat, QQ, DingTalk, Slack, or another app's built-in browser. Those apps block the microphone for every web page — it is their restriction, not something a page can work around. Use their ⋯ menu and choose Open in browser.
  • Allow microphone access when the browser asks. You can re-grant it in your browser's site settings.
  • On iOS, Safari ends the listening session every so often; the page restarts it by itself. If it ever stops for good, tap the microphone button once to start again.

Pairing will not complete

  • The pairing request expires after three minutes — tap Pair again on your phone, then click Allow on your computer.
  • Make sure Chrome is running on the computer where you installed the extension, and that the extension badge is not red.
  • If you previously saved a relay address in the extension's Settings page, clear it — otherwise the extension keeps looking for a relay on your own computer instead of the cloud one.

The extension says "Disconnected"

  • It reconnects on its own within a few seconds. If it does not, click Reconnect in the popup.
  • Check that your computer has internet access, and that a firewall or VPN is not blocking relay.micpilot.tech.

AI features and the DeepSeek API key

  • Around 60 everyday commands work with no key at all. The key only adds the smarter understanding of casual phrasing, page summaries, and spoken answers.
  • Create a key at platform.deepseek.com — note that it is a separate account from the DeepSeek chat app.
  • The key is stored in your own browser and is never sent to us. If a key check fails, the popup and the phone page both tell you why (invalid, no balance, network).
